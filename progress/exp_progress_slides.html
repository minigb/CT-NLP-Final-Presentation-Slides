Symbolic-Fused LALM Progress

Symbolic-Aware Large Audio Language Models

Experiment journey toward a learned S_plan token for UniAudio-style audio language modeling.

Goal

Learn compact symbolic tokens that carry music structure and help audio-token modeling.

Current status

S_plan is a learned symbolic planning codec with acoustic-token utility and MIR-recoverable structure.

Not yet

Not yet S_LLM LLM-supervised tokenizer training; not yet final LALM music reasoning.

Notation: S_plan is the current learned symbolic planning token; S_LLM is the planned LLM-supervised symbolic tokenizer/token stream and has not been trained yet.

Prepared for team research discussion | 2026-06-12
One-sentence objective

Make symbolic music useful inside an audio language model

We test whether low-rate symbolic planning tokens can reduce acoustic-token uncertainty and expose recoverable music structure under controlled aligned-vs-corrupted comparisons.

What we can say now

  • Frozen audio tokens contain recoverable MIR information.
  • S_plan is compact and reconstructive.
  • Aligned S_plan improves proxy acoustic-token modeling.
  • MIR structure is recoverable from frozen S_plan.

What we cannot claim yet

  • Full symbolic reasoning token.
  • S_LLM LLM-supervised tokenizer training.
  • Robust final-LALM open-ended music QA.
  • Generation/control intervention success.

Why not start with QA?

Frozen UniAudio2 QA is too brittle for the main claim

Diagnostic setup
  • Released UniAudio2 stack.
  • ReasoningCodec.checkpoint + llm_ep2.checkpoint.
  • Slakh test primary instrument-presence QA.
  • 151 clips, 906 QA rows.
Observed behavior
  • Exact accuracy 0.6181.
  • Macro-F1 0.3782.
  • Strict invalid rate 0.3400.
  • Predictions: 796 yes, 3 no, 107 invalid.

Discussion: this motivates structured token-level and MIR evaluations with objective labels and controls.

Missing: robust open-ended music QA is not yet a completed claim.

Representation motivation

Audio tokens already carry music information

Frozen-token MIR probes asked whether reason and semantic tokens encode recoverable instrument and structure labels.

These probes are not the final contribution, but they justify token-level evaluation before symbolic fusion.

Design lesson
  • Use token-level information recovery.
  • Keep label-shuffle and feature-shuffle controls.
  • Avoid relying only on final free-form QA.
  • Preserve track-level splits and metadata provenance.

EXP031B

What S_LLM tokenization with LLM will actually mean

Planned objective

Train planned S_LLM tokens through a separate text decoder on deterministic MIR captions, symbolic QA, and structured music text targets.

Planned setup

  • Llama-3.2-compatible token-training decoder.
  • 300M-scale debug/ablation first.
  • 3B-compatible faithful check later.
  • Qwen2.5/Qwen3 as cross-family ablation.

Planned, not yet run. EXP031B S_LLM should wait until EXP031A controls establish that aligned S_plan carries causal structure.

Current paper story

If controls pass, the story is clean

We learn a compact symbolic planning token from MIDI-derived structure, show that it reduces acoustic-token uncertainty beyond corrupted symbolic controls, show that naive LALM insertion fails, introduce a UniAudio-style symbolic stream interface with aligned-control gains, and verify through MIR-supervised controls that the symbolic bottleneck preserves recoverable music structure.

Contribution shape

  • Tokenizer design: compact symbolic planning codec.
  • Utility evidence: acoustic-token CE/PPL reductions.
  • Interface evidence: UniAudio-style stream controls.
  • Representation evidence: MIR-supervised recovery.

If controls fail

Then the story narrows: current S_plan is useful as reconstruction/acoustic proxy, but MIR recovery may involve priors or shortcuts. The planned S_LLM objective must be revised before LLM supervision.
